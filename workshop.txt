title=10 Years Later Animal Spawn Fix
description=Fixes the game kicking you to the main menu with this error when playing with [b]10 YEARS LATER [Unofficial][/b] on Build 42:
description=[code]Cannot read field "turnDelta" because "this.adef" is null at IsoAnimal.update[/code]
description=
description=[h2]What's going on[/h2]
description=10 Years Later spawns animals on outdoor tiles as the map loads, but doesn't check for water. When an animal gets spawned on a water tile, the game adds it to the world and then stops setting it up, leaving an uninitialized animal. The next time the game updates it, it crashes.
description=
description=[h2]What this does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Blocks animal spawns on water tiles (those would end up broken anyway)
description=[*]Removes any animal that still comes back uninitialized
description=[*]Logs blocked spawns to the console, tagged [TYLAnimalSpawnFix]
description=[/list]
description=Everything else spawns as normal. The game's own animal spawning isn't touched.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Build 42 (made on 42.21)
description=[*]Single player and multiplayer (runs on the server)
description=[*]Safe to add or remove mid-save
description=[*]Load order doesn't matter
description=[/list]
